Witness of Makwengura

777 · 999 · 333 MAKWENGURA MASAWI

This ring is a public prayer and witness for every descendant named from Makwengura. It joins the old language of the House with a simple moving geometry — three circles, three numbers, one Name.

Unwavering faith is asked of Mwari ndi Mwari, not of a screen. Light, proportion, and repetition are used here the way a hymn is used: to keep a household in mind. Ideas from physics (field, wave, coherence) are spoken as metaphor. They are not a laboratory claim that a website can cancel harm.

A page cannot eat a curse. God, elders, medicine, and the law remain first. While this page stands it only repeats the blessing: let the House live in peace.
